# Bullet Journal
## Introduction
The purpose of this application is to build a digital version of a Bullet Journal, which is a more structured approach to organising a to-do list, 
and keeping a log of tasks, events, and notes to look back on and reflect.
  - [Bullet Journal Site FAQs](https://bulletjournal.com/blogs/faq)
  - [How to Bullet Journal - YoutTube Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fm15cmYU0IM)
## How A Bullet Journal Works
The to-do section of the journal is divided up into 3 main sections:
1.  Future Log
2.  Monthly Log
3.  Daily Log

• Tasks, ○ Events, and - Notes are logged into these sections and the sections work together to help with planning time ahead.
As we move through the months, we tick items off our list, cross them out, or bring them forwards.
Migrating items forwards in the journal allows an opportunity to reflect on whether they are worth moving forwards or crossing out, why they weren't completed, etc.
